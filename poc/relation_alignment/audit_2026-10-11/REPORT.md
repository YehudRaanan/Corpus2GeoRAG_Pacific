# WarSampo ↔ Wikidata relation alignment: executed audit

Data retrieved: 2026-10-10 22:29–22:39 UTC. Ranking rerun at 23:02 UTC from cached responses. This audits relation **types** on shared Finnish WWII objects. It does not import facts into the Pacific graph. EventKG was not needed for this two-source test.

## Scope and method

The reproducible collector is [`../run_two_source_audit.py`](../run_two_source_audit.py). It queried the [WarSampo SPARQL endpoint](https://ldf.fi/warsa/sparql), [Wikidata entity API](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_access), and Wikipedia page properties. It inventoried every WarSampo IRI-to-IRI predicate (145), then built identity candidates from 159 direct Wikidata-linked WarSampo actors, 488 WarSampo unit resources with Wikipedia pages, and five manually checked war/period links. The Wikidata side covered all item-valued claims on those candidate items (117 properties). The snapshot is a retrieval window, not an atomic dump: Wikidata item revisions are in `identity_candidates.csv`; WarSampo has no recorded graph revision.

`identity_candidates.csv` contains 576 candidate links for 572 WarSampo resources. Four WarSampo resources have multiple QID candidates. The identity screen classified **7 reviewed**, **243 screened**, **323 unresolved**, and **3 rejected** links. “Screened” means a direct person link with human type and a shared name token, or a unit Wikipedia link with a matching Finnish name and compatible unit type. It is a filter for candidate evidence, **not a final historical identity judgment**. The 250 reviewed or screened resources yielded 202 distinct mapped relation triples (predicate and QID endpoints). The wider unreviewed crosswalk yielded 326. The two categories must not be merged.

The rejected links include WarSampo's individual boat [`actor_2245`](http://ldf.fi/warsa/actors/actor_2245) → Wikidata's [Sisu boat class, Q2332854](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q2332854), [`actor_2242`](http://ldf.fi/warsa/actors/actor_2242) → [Isku boat class, Q4846250](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q4846250), and [`actor_313`](http://ldf.fi/warsa/actors/actor_313) → [a Wikimedia category, Q10240708](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q10240708). Renumbered military units remain unresolved. The five period links were checked by names and date intervals. [Lapland War](http://ldf.fi/warsa/conflicts/LaplandWar) and [Interim Peace](http://ldf.fi/warsa/conflicts/InterimPeace) have small endpoint-date differences between sources; do not merge their date claims.

For the seven observed combinations: **1 accepted, 1 related, 2 rejected, 3 unresolved**. The three extra definition-based candidates remain unresolved. The five checked `hasConflict` examples and all three `hasUpperUnit` pairs had compatible endpoint identities, but only `hasConflict` passed the predicate-definition review. None of the shared Wikidata statements had P580/P582/P585 time qualifiers; dates are attributes of claims or events, never a new relation type.

The 100% results have only two or three shared pairs; `hasConflict` ↔ `P607` has the strongest repeated-pair support.

## Success check and limits

**250 screened or reviewed WarSampo identities** yielded **202 eligible mapped relation triples**. Of those, **122 distinct endpoint pairs** had at least one Wikidata predicate on the same two items: 115 unit/conflict, 3 unit/unit hierarchy, 3 war/WWII, and 1 non-identity/succession pair. These are not 122 accepted mappings. **One relation-type mapping passed review** (`hasConflict` ↔ `P607` in the stated scope). The 115 supporting pairs are screened candidates, not 115 fully checked fact transfers.

This is an executed **bounded overlap audit**, not a claim of complete two-source coverage. The 323 unresolved identity links can hide more matches. The WarSampo endpoint has **154 typed Event resources**, of which **0 have a direct Wikidata `owl:sameAs` link** and **0 have a Wikipedia `foaf:page` link**. Places also lack verified crosswalks here. The collector enumerated all 145 WarSampo IRI predicates but could compare only pairs with both endpoints linked; it inventoried Wikidata outgoing claims on linked candidate items, not all incoming claims globally. Thus the complete-coverage gate in the [mapping process](../../../docs/WARSAMPO_WIKIDATA_RELATION_MAPPING_PROCESS.md) remains open. The next material work is to verify unresolved military-unit identities and link *the same* event/place objects before reviewing P11/P710 and P7/P276.

## Reproduction and evidence files

Run `python poc/relation_alignment/run_two_source_audit.py`. The output directory contains `manifest.json`, all 145 WarSampo IRI predicates in `warsampo_predicates.csv`, all 576 identity candidates with screening reasons and Wikidata revisions in `identity_candidates.csv`, all 117 Wikidata item-valued predicates in `wikidata_predicates.csv`, and both provisional and screened overlap tables with source URIs, QIDs, statement IDs, ranks, qualifier IDs, and reference counts. Re-running the script uses its temporary HTTP cache; remove that cache or change the output date for a fresh source retrieval.
